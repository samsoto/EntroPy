How the math works & what it cannot tell us

Uncertainty in the chosen outcome: — bits. A fair coin has one bit; an outcome that never changes has zero.

    Each clue is considered separately. A relationship can be coincidence, a shared cause, or a consequence; this analysis cannot distinguish them. Repeated hours are not independent experiments. Confirm patterns on new data before relying on them.

    THE 30-SECOND VERSION

    It’s a pattern finder.

    Imagine a list of rainy and dry hours. Could knowing the cloud cover help you tell them apart? What about wind or temperature?

    EntroPy compares those clues and tells you which one carries the most information. Then it shows actual counts, so you can see what the result means.

    1. Pick a city or story
    We fetch recent weather automatically. Sam’s historical stock study and a made-up machine example are also included.
    2. Read the takeaway
    It names the strongest clue in the sample. Click any bar to see a concrete example.
    3. Try changing something
    Choose another city, or open “What changed?” to compare with the earlier period. No files or math background needed.

    This is an exploration of patterns, not a forecast. Same-hour weather relationships and same-day stock relationships do not establish what will happen next.
